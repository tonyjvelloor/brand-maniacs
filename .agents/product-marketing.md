# TBM International Strategy & Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-09-02

This document serves as the master plan for The Brand Maniacs' international expansion. It is referenced by all agentic skills (SEO, Copywriting, Ads) to ensure consistent global positioning.

---

## 1. Global Positioning
- **The Core Identity:** A globally positioned growth studio, operating from India.
- **What We Do:** We build growth systems for ambitious brands and products. We do not sell isolated "services" (SEO, design, etc.).
- **The Narrative:** We don't just market businesses. We build the infrastructure that helps them grow. 
- **The Advantage:** "Built in India. Designed for the world. Global ambition. Indian engineering discipline." We turn our location into a strategic strength (engineering rigor, scaling capability) rather than competing as a cheap offshore agency.

## 2. Ideal International Client
- **Profile:** Ambitious brands, tech companies, SaaS, and D2C businesses.
- **Mindset:** They want a strategic growth partner, not an order-taker. They understand that marketing is a behavior science and systems engineering problem.
- **Anti-Persona:** Companies looking for cheap offshore execution or isolated tactical deliverables without strategy.

## 3. First 3 Target Countries
We are deliberately NOT targeting "worldwide" initially.
1. **UAE (🇦🇪):** Priority #1. Proximity, strong business ecosystem, high-value businesses.
2. **UK (🇬🇧):** Priority #2. Large English-speaking market + strong SME/startup ecosystem.
3. **Australia (🇦🇺):** Priority #3. English-speaking, strong SMB/D2C opportunity, manageable timezone.
*(Note: USA is Priority #4 for the long-term; Singapore is Priority #5).*

## 4. Industries to Attack
- **SaaS & Digital Products:** Showcasing internal builds like FlexPilot and CouponHub.
- **E-Commerce / D2C:** High-growth consumer brands needing full-funnel acquisition systems.
- **B2B / Tech Infrastructure:** Companies requiring robust positioning, creative, and performance funnels.

## 5. International Service / Offer Ladder
We sell systems, structured in an ascending commitment funnel:
- **Free/Low-Friction:** Growth Assessment.
- **Paid (Entry Point): Growth Architecture (2 weeks)**
  - Audit the business → identify constraints → build opportunity map → produce strategic roadmap.
- **Core: Growth System Build (6-12 weeks)**
  - Strategy + creative + digital + acquisition infrastructure.
  - Sub-systems: Brand Foundation, Attention Engine, Conversion Engine, Growth Engine, AI & Intelligence.
- **Recurring: Growth Partner (Ongoing)**
  - Experimentation + creative + performance + optimization + intelligence.

## 7. Website Architecture
The site must instantly communicate what we do, not where we are.
- **Hierarchy:** Hero (We Build Growth Systems) → Proof (Brands, Products, Systems) → Global Positioning (Built in India, Designed for the world).
- **Global Authority Pages:** `/growth-systems`, `/creative-growth`, `/digital-products`, `/performance-marketing`, `/ai-automation`, `/method`, `/work`
- **Future Commercial Pages (No SEO spam):** `/locations/uae`, `/locations/uk`, `/locations/australia` (only when genuinely useful market strategies exist).

## 8. SEO / AEO / GEO Architecture
- **Focus:** Build authority around Growth Systems, methodology, original frameworks, and AI-search visibility.
- **Case Study Structure (The "System" Format):**
  - CLIENT | MARKET | PROBLEM
  - WHAT WE BUILT: Brand + acquisition + digital infrastructure
  - SYSTEM: Strategy → Creative → Website → Performance → Measurement
  - OUTCOME: Documented business outcomes
  - **LEARNING:** What TBM learned that applies beyond this particular client (highly transferable IP).

## 9. LinkedIn / Founder Acquisition System
- **Channel:** Tony's LinkedIn presence is the primary distribution channel for TBM internationally.
- **Content Strategy:** Intellectual property and systems thinking.
  - *Example topics:* "Why most brands don't have a marketing problem—they have a growth-system problem."
  - "We rebuilt a company's acquisition system. Here's what we learned."
  - "Why your performance marketing agency shouldn't be designing your brand strategy."

## 10. 90-Day Plan to Land the First International Client
*The International Flywheel Model:* Content → Expert Positioning → International Traffic → Growth Audit → Architecture Project → System Build → Recurring Partnership → Case Study → Referrals/SEO/AI → More Clients.

- **Phase A (Positioning):** Finalize this document (pricing, exact ideal customer).
